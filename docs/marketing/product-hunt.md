## Respostas prontas

Use com as suas palavras; a regra é responder em até 30 minutos e sempre com uma pergunta de volta.

**"Como é diferente do Gamma?"**
Both generate a deck from a brief. The difference is what happens after: Mostra gives you a free-canvas editor (layers, groups, guides, auto layout, like Figma) instead of blocks, pastes Figma/Illustrator as editable layers, has three formats (Slides, scroll Story, free-navigation Explore), 3D and Lottie, and private links with password. And it's priced in BRL for the Brazilian market. What do you miss most in Gamma today?

**"Exporta PPTX?"**
PDF yes (one page per screen, fonts embedded). PPTX no — Mostra reads an old .pptx theme to build your brand kit, but the presentation lives at the link, with animations a file can't carry. Would PPTX export be a blocker for you, or is PDF enough?

**"Quanto custa?"**
Free forever: 50 credits and 3 projects. Plus R$29/month (150 credits), Pro R$74 (400), Studio R$149 per member (800, shared). Annual is 20% off. Credit packs (100 for R$19, 400 for R$69, 2000 for R$299) never expire. 1 credit = 1 AI-designed screen; editing, presenting and sharing are always free.

**"Funciona em inglês?"**
Yes. The AI writes in the language of your brief, and the editor has no language barrier. The site and support are Portuguese-first.

**"Tem colaboração?"**
Yes, real-time editing in the Studio plan (shared credits, shared library). Lower plans are single-seat with private links for reviewers.

**"Os dados do meu cliente ficam onde?"**
[preencher: região de hospedagem, retenção e política de privacidade quando a página existir]. Links can be password-protected, set to expire and revoked at any time.

**"Tem API?"**
API access is part of the Pro plan [preencher o que a API faz hoje antes de responder com detalhe].

**Crítica dura ("mais um gerador de slides com IA")**
Fair — there are many. The bet here is on the editor: AI gets you 80% of the way and then you need real tools (layers, auto layout, pasting from Figma) to finish, not another text box. Try it with a real brief and tell me where it broke.
